Quick Answers

Do you offer 1-on-1 trading coaching or mentorship?

Not at this time. All of our educational content is published for free on our website and YouTube channel. We believe the best coaching is the content itself — and it's available to everyone without cost.

Can you analyze a specific coin for me?

We cover coin requests from time to time on our YouTube channel and Telegram group. The best way to request a coin analysis is to join our Telegram community @cryp_talk13 and post your request there — community votes help us decide what to cover next.

How do I report an error or outdated content?

Please email us at tolkcryp@gmail.com with the subject line "Content Error" and include the URL of the page and a brief description of the issue. We take accuracy seriously and will review and update content promptly.
