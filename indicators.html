Volume Profile Pro

Point of Control Value Area HVN Real-time

Volume Profile Pro visualizes the exact price levels where the most trading volume has occurred over any selected lookback period. Unlike standard volume bars (which show volume per candle), this indicator plots a horizontal histogram showing the volume traded at each price node — giving you an instant visual map of where the market has shown the most interest.

The Point of Control (POC) — the price level with the highest traded volume — acts as the market's gravitational center and is statistically one of the most reliable support/resistance zones in all of technical analysis. The Value Area (VA) contains 70% of all volume and defines the "fair value" price range. Breakouts above the Value Area High or below the Value Area Low frequently lead to explosive, directional moves. Use this indicator across any timeframe for identifying institutional demand and supply zones on BTC, ETH, SOL, and all major crypto pairs.

Smart Money Confluence

Order Blocks Fair Value Gaps Liquidity Zones

Smart Money Confluence (SMC) is our most advanced indicator, implementing the institutional trading concepts popularized by Inner Circle Trader (ICT) methodology, adapted specifically for cryptocurrency markets. It automatically identifies three critical market structures that professional "smart money" traders exploit daily.

Order Blocks are identified as the last bullish/bearish candle before a significant move, representing zones where institutional orders were placed and are likely to be revisited. Fair Value Gaps (FVGs) are inefficiencies left in price action where the market moved too fast — these gaps act as price magnets and frequently get "filled" before the trend resumes. Liquidity Zones map areas above swing highs and below swing lows where stop-loss orders cluster — ideal targets for institutional traders to execute large orders.

This indicator works best on 1-hour, 4-hour, and daily charts for BTC, ETH, and SOL. It paints all three structures simultaneously, giving you a complete picture of the institutional order flow before entering any trade.

Trend Signal Elite

Multi-TF Trend Entry Signals Dynamic SL/TP

Trend Signal Elite is our flagship trend-following indicator, designed to solve one of the most common trader problems: entering trends too late or exiting too early. It uses a proprietary multi-timeframe alignment algorithm that checks three higher timeframes simultaneously before generating any signal, dramatically reducing false entries during choppy, ranging market conditions.

When all three timeframes align in the same direction, the indicator plots a directional arrow (▲ Buy or ▼ Sell) on your chart and colors the background to clearly communicate the active trend state. The dynamic trailing stop-loss automatically adjusts as price moves in your favor — locking in profits while remaining wide enough not to get stopped out by normal market noise.

The built-in risk-to-reward overlay visually draws the take-profit zone at 2R and 3R levels from every entry signal, so you always know your targets before entering a trade. Compatible with all crypto pairs and timeframes.

Common Questions

Indicator FAQ

Do I need a paid TradingView plan to use these indicators?

No. All three Cryp Tolk indicators are published as "public scripts" on TradingView and can be added to any account — including the free Basic plan. However, a TradingView Pro or Pro+ plan allows you to run multiple indicators simultaneously on the same chart, which is ideal if you want to combine Volume Profile Pro with Smart Money Confluence and Trend Signal Elite at once.

Which cryptocurrency pairs work best with these indicators?

All three indicators are optimized for high-liquidity perpetual futures and spot pairs. We recommend using them on BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, DOGEUSDT, and BNBUSDT on Binance or Bybit. Lower-cap altcoins may produce less reliable signals due to thinner order books and higher susceptibility to market manipulation. For best results, apply the indicators on the 4-hour or daily timeframe first to establish directional bias, then drop to 1-hour for entries.

How often are the indicators updated?

We actively maintain and update all three scripts. When TradingView releases Pine Script language updates, we revise the indicators to ensure compatibility and take advantage of new features. Volume Profile Pro and Trend Signal Elite each receive a major update every quarter. If you add the scripts to your favorites on TradingView, any updates are automatically applied to your charts — no action needed on your end.
