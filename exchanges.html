Frequently Asked Questions

Which exchange is best for complete beginners?

For beginners, we recommend starting with Binance for spot trading due to its simple interface and enormous range of educational resources, or Bitget if you're interested in copy trading — where you can automatically mirror the trades of profitable traders while you're still learning. Both have strong mobile apps and extensive support.

Is it safe to leave funds on these exchanges?

All four exchanges employ industry-standard security — cold storage for the majority of user funds, two-factor authentication (2FA), anti-phishing measures, and insurance/protection funds. However, the general crypto principle "not your keys, not your coins" still applies. For long-term holdings, consider withdrawing to a hardware wallet (Ledger, Trezor). For active trading accounts, keeping funds on a reputable exchange is considered acceptable.

Can I sign up for more than one exchange?

Absolutely. Most experienced traders use at least two exchanges — typically one for spot accumulation (Binance is popular for this) and one for leveraged futures trading (Bybit is the community favourite). There's no rule against signing up for all four and collecting all four bonuses simultaneously.

What's the minimum deposit to activate a bonus?

Minimum deposit thresholds vary by exchange and promotion. Bybit's $30,000 bonus typically requires graduated deposits and trading volumes to unlock progressively. Binance's 20% referral fee discount is applied automatically and requires no minimum deposit — just signing up through our link activates it for life. Check each exchange's current terms for exact minimums, as these can change with promotions.
